Here is a comprehensive list of the **Top 50 System Design Interview Questions**, specifically curated for professionals with **3–5 years of experience**. These questions strike a balance between **foundational knowledge**, **scalability concerns**, **real-world architecture**, and **trade-off analysis**—commonly asked in product-based companies.

---

## ✅ Top 50 System Design Interview Questions (3–5 Years Experience)

---

### ⚙️ **1–10: Fundamentals of System Design**

1. What is system design and why is it important?
2. What is the difference between High-Level Design (HLD) and Low-Level Design (LLD)?
3. What is scalability? Explain horizontal vs vertical scaling.
4. What is latency vs throughput?
5. What is load balancing and how does it work?
6. What is a CDN (Content Delivery Network) and why is it used?
7. What are stateless and stateful systems?
8. What are CAP theorem and its implications?
9. What is the difference between monolithic and microservice architectures?
10. What is the role of caching in system design?

---

### ⚙️ **11–20: Databases & Storage**

11. When do you use SQL vs NoSQL?
12. What are the types of NoSQL databases?
13. What is sharding? What are the pros and cons?
14. What is database replication? Explain master-slave and master-master.
15. How do you handle data consistency across distributed systems?
16. What is eventual consistency?
17. How do you design a highly available data store?
18. What is the difference between ACID and BASE properties?
19. What are indexes in databases and how do they improve performance?
20. How would you design a rate-limiting mechanism for an API?

---

### ⚙️ **21–30: Architecture & Components**

21. What is a microservices architecture? What are its pros and cons?
22. What is service discovery in microservices?
23. How do you design a service registry like Netflix Eureka?
24. What is an API gateway and how does it fit in system design?
25. How do you handle inter-service communication?
26. What is the role of message queues in system design (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ)?
27. What is eventual consistency and how do you achieve it?
28. How do you design for fault tolerance and resilience?
29. What is circuit breaker pattern and why is it used?
30. What is a saga pattern in microservices?

---

### ⚙️ **31–40: Scalability, Caching & Performance**

31. How do you scale a database?
32. How would you design a URL shortening service like Bitly?
33. How would you design a cache invalidation strategy?
34. What is the difference between Redis and Memcached?
35. What are consistent hashing and its use cases?
36. How do you design a distributed cache?
37. How do you design a file upload service (e.g., Google Drive)?
38. How would you scale a system to handle 10 million users?
39. How do you monitor performance and detect bottlenecks in your system?
40. How do you handle schema evolution in a distributed system?

---

### ⚙️ **41–50: Real-world Problems & Trade-offs**

41. Design a social media feed system like Facebook or Twitter.
42. How would you design a notification system?
43. Design a chat application like WhatsApp.
44. How would you design a video streaming platform like YouTube?
45. How do you handle authentication and session management at scale?
46. How would you design a recommendation system (like Netflix/Amazon)?
47. How do you secure sensitive data at rest and in transit?
48. How would you design a global payment system?
49. What are the trade-offs between consistency, availability, and partition tolerance?
50. How do you approach designing a system from scratch in an interview?
