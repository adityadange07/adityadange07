Here are the **Top 50 System Design Interview Questions** tailored for someone with **1 year of experience**. At this level, interviews typically focus on **basic system design principles, components, and problem-solving ability**, rather than deep distributed systems.

## ✅ Top 50 System Design Interview Questions (1 Year Experience)

### 🔹 1. Basic Concepts

1. **What is system design?**
2. **What is the difference between high-level and low-level design?**
3. **What are the main components of a scalable system?**
4. **What is latency vs throughput?**

7. **What is a CDN and how does it work?**
