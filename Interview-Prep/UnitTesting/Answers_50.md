## ✅ Top 50 Java Unit Testing Interview Questions

---

### 🔹 JUnit Fundamentals (1–10)

1. **What is unit testing and why is it important?**
2. **Explain the basic structure of a JUnit test class.**
3. **What are the key annotations used in JUnit 4 and JUnit 5?**
4. **How is `@BeforeEach` different from `@BeforeAll` in JUnit 5?**
5. **How do you write a parameterized test in JUnit?**
6. **What is the use of `@Disabled` annotation?**
7. **How do you test exceptions in JUnit 5?**
8. **What are `assertEquals()`, `assertTrue()`, and `assertThrows()` used for?**
9. **What is the execution lifecycle of a JUnit test class?**
10. **How do you organize test cases in a large project?**

### 🔹 Mockito & Mocking (11–20)

11. **What is Mockito and why is it used in unit testing?**
12. **What is the difference between `@Mock` and `@Spy`?**
13. **What is the purpose of `@InjectMocks` in Mockito?**
14. **How do you stub a method in Mockito?**
15. **How do you verify a method invocation with Mockito?**
16. **How do you mock a void method in Mockito?**
17. **What is `ArgumentCaptor` in Mockito and when would you use it?**
18. **How do you mock final classes or static methods in Mockito?**
19. **What are some common Mockito errors and how do you debug them?**
20. **How do you reset mocks between test methods?**
