## ✅ Top 50 DevOps Interview Questions (3–5 Years Experience)

### 🔹 Section 1: DevOps Basics & Concepts

1. **What is DevOps and how does it benefit organizations?**
2. **How is DevOps different from Agile?**
3. **What are the main phases of the DevOps lifecycle?**
4. **What is the role of CI/CD in DevOps?**
5. **What is shift-left testing?**
6. **What is the “Infrastructure as Code” concept in DevOps?**
7. **What are the key components of a DevOps pipeline?**
8. **Which DevOps tools are you most experienced with?**
9. **What is version control, and which systems have you used?**
10. **How do you ensure DevOps automation and consistency across environments?**

### 🔹 Section 2: CI/CD & Jenkins

11. **What is Jenkins, and how have you used it in your projects?**
12. **What’s the difference between a Jenkins freestyle job and a pipeline?**
13. **How do you manage credentials securely in Jenkins?**
14. **What plugins have you used in Jenkins?**
15. **How can you trigger builds automatically in Jenkins?**
16. **What is the difference between declarative and scripted pipelines in Jenkins?**
17. **How do you integrate testing in a Jenkins pipeline?**
18. **Have you worked with GitHub Actions or GitLab CI/CD? How do they compare to Jenkins?**
19. **What’s your approach for rollback strategies in CI/CD?**
20. **How do you manage artifacts in your build pipeline?**

21. **What is Docker, and how does it work in a DevOps environment?**

23. **What is a Dockerfile and what are its key instructions?**

26. **What are multi-stage Docker builds and why are they useful?**
27. **How do you optimize Docker images?**
28. **How do you handle security for Docker containers?**

30. **How do you debug a non-working Docker container?**

40. **What logging and monitoring tools have you used with Kubernetes?**
