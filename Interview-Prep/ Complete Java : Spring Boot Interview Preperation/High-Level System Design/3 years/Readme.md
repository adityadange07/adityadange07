Here are the **Top 50 High-Level System Design Interview Questions** tailored for **3 years of experience**, ideal for **mid-level software engineers** expected to have a good understanding of architecture, scalability, trade-offs, and common design patterns.

---

## ✅ Top 50 High-Level System Design Interview Questions (3 Years Experience)

These questions focus on architecture, communication, trade-offs, scaling strategies, and real-world systems — without getting too deep into distributed algorithms or database internals.

---

### 🔹 1. Fundamentals

1. What is system design, and why is it important?
2. What is the difference between high-level and low-level system design?
3. What are some key principles of good system design?
4. How do you start designing a new system from scratch?
5. How do you identify and define functional and non-functional requirements?

---

### 🔹 2. Architecture Patterns

6. What is a monolithic architecture?
7. What is a microservices architecture? What are its advantages and disadvantages?
8. What is service-oriented architecture (SOA)?
9. What is the difference between a layered architecture and event-driven architecture?
10. How would you break a monolith into microservices?

---

### 🔹 3. Scalability & Performance

11. What is horizontal vs vertical scaling?
12. How do you handle a sudden spike in traffic?
13. What is a load balancer and how does it work?
14. What are bottlenecks in a system, and how do you detect them?
15. How do you handle performance degradation in high-load systems?

---

### 🔹 4. Caching

16. What is caching, and why is it important?
17. Where in a system can caching be implemented?
18. What is cache invalidation, and why is it hard?
19. Explain write-through vs write-back caching.
20. What is a CDN and how does it improve performance?

---

### 🔹 5. Databases (High-Level)

21. What is the difference between SQL and NoSQL?
22. When would you choose a NoSQL database over SQL?
23. What is database sharding?
24. What is database replication? What are its types?
25. How do you ensure high availability in your database?

---

### 🔹 6. Communication & APIs

26. What is REST API? What are its key principles?
27. How would you design a versioning strategy for APIs?
28. What is the difference between synchronous and asynchronous communication?
29. What is gRPC and when would you use it?
30. How would you ensure backward compatibility in APIs?

---

### 🔹 7. Reliability & Fault Tolerance

31. How do you design for high availability?
32. What is a single point of failure, and how do you eliminate it?
33. What is a retry mechanism and when should you use it?
34. How would you handle failures in a distributed system?
35. How do you implement health checks in a system?

---

### 🔹 8. Security & Data Privacy

36. How do you secure data in transit and at rest?
37. How would you handle user authentication and authorization in a distributed system?
38. What is the principle of least privilege?
39. How would you protect a system from DDoS attacks?
40. What strategies can be used to protect sensitive user data?

---

### 🔹 9. Real-World Design Questions

41. How would you design a URL shortener like bit.ly?
42. How would you design a real-time chat system?
43. How would you design a news feed like Facebook or Twitter?
44. How would you design a notification system?
45. How would you design a file upload service (like Google Drive)?

---

### 🔹 10. Miscellaneous & Best Practices

46. What is the CAP theorem? Give real-world examples.
47. What are trade-offs between consistency and availability?
48. What is eventual consistency? When is it acceptable?
49. What is the role of a message queue in system design?
50. What are some tools and services used for monitoring and alerting?
