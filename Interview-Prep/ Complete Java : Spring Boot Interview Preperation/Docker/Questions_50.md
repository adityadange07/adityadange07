## ✅ Top 50 Docker Interview Questions (3–5 Years Experience)

### 🔹 1–10: Docker Basics & Concepts

1. What is Docker, and how does it differ from a virtual machine?

3. What are Docker images and containers? How do they work?
4. What is the purpose of a Dockerfile?
5. What are the key components of Docker architecture?

10. What is the role of the `ENTRYPOINT` and `CMD` instructions in a Dockerfile?
