Here are the **Top 100 HR Round Interview Questions** tailored for a **Java Software Developer with 3.5 years of experience**, covering personal background, professional experience, behavioral scenarios, team collaboration, goals, ethics, and job fit.

---

### 🔹 **1–20: Personal & General Background**

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Walk me through your resume.
3. Why did you choose Java as your primary technology?
4. Why do you want to leave your current company?
5. Why do you want to join our organization?
6. What do you know about our company?
7. What are your key strengths?
8. What are your weaknesses?
9. How do you stay updated with the latest Java technologies?
10. How do you manage work-life balance?
11. Tell us about your educational background.
12. What inspired you to become a software developer?
13. How do you handle pressure or tight deadlines?
14. Where do you see yourself in the next 5 years?
15. What motivates you at work?
16. Are you open to relocation?
17. Are you comfortable with hybrid or remote work?
18. How do you prioritize your tasks during peak workload?
19. Describe your ideal work environment.
20. What is your current CTC and expected CTC?

---

### 🔹 **21–40: Professional Experience**

21. Describe your role in the current project.
22. What technologies have you worked with besides Java?
23. How do you handle production issues?
24. Have you mentored juniors or peers?
25. What was the biggest technical challenge you’ve faced?
26. How do you contribute to code reviews?
27. What is your experience with agile/scrum methodology?
28. What do you do when a project scope changes?
29. Have you worked on client-facing roles or demos?
30. What tools do you use for code quality or version control?
31. Describe a time you missed a deadline. What did you do?
32. How do you ensure your code is secure?
33. Tell me about a successful project and your contribution.
34. Have you ever failed in a task? How did you respond?
35. How do you handle repetitive tasks?
36. What are your contributions in unit testing?
37. How do you work with cross-functional teams (QA, DevOps)?
38. Have you written any documentation for your project?
39. What deployment processes are you familiar with?
40. What is your daily development workflow?

---

### 🔹 **41–60: Teamwork & Communication**

41. How do you handle team conflicts?
42. Do you prefer working independently or in a team?
43. How do you handle feedback or criticism?
44. Describe a time when you helped a teammate.
45. What if a team member is not cooperating?
46. How do you resolve disagreements on design choices?
47. How do you keep your team updated on your progress?
48. Have you led a team or module before?
49. How do you deal with underperforming team members?
50. How do you ensure effective communication with your manager?
51. How do you handle overlapping responsibilities?
52. Describe a time you had to explain a complex technical issue.
53. What role do you usually take in a team?
54. How do you ensure code consistency in a team?
55. Have you faced a situation where your team missed a goal?
56. What does a healthy team culture mean to you?
57. How do you balance coding and team collaboration?
58. Have you been part of any code refactoring initiatives?
59. How do you handle passive-aggressive behavior in the team?
60. How do you appreciate or recognize your peers?

---

### 🔹 **61–80: Ethics, Integrity & Decision Making**

61. Have you ever disagreed with your manager?
62. Have you ever made a mistake in production?
63. What would you do if you discovered a serious bug after release?
64. How do you handle confidential data or IP-sensitive work?
65. What’s your approach when you're asked to cut corners?
66. What would you do if you’re asked to work overtime without pay?
67. What will you do if you’re offered a counteroffer?
68. How do you decide when to escalate an issue?
69. What would you do if you’re given unclear instructions?
70. What’s more important: quality or speed?
71. How do you stay honest and accountable in a team?
72. What would you do if you saw someone else taking credit for your work?
73. Have you ever faced an ethical dilemma at work?
74. What’s your definition of professionalism?
75. Would you work on a project you don’t morally agree with?
76. Do you believe in transparency at work?
77. Have you ever reported unethical behavior?
78. Do you believe rules can be bent for project success?
79. How do you handle conflicting priorities from two managers?
80. Do you speak up when you see something wrong?

---

### 🔹 **81–100: Job Fit, Goals & Closing**

81. What are you looking for in your next role?
82. How do you plan to grow technically?
83. What are your long-term career goals?
84. How do you upskill outside of work?
85. Are you planning higher studies or certifications?
86. How do you manage your time during job transitions?
87. Are you interviewing with other companies?
88. What excites you the most about this opportunity?
89. How do you plan to contribute in your first 90 days here?
90. What kind of manager helps you perform your best?
91. What kind of projects are you most interested in?
92. Why should we hire you?
93. What is your biggest achievement in the last 3 years?
94. How do you define success?
95. How do you handle failure?
96. What kind of feedback do you get in appraisals?
97. Have you ever negotiated a job offer?
98. When can you join us?
99. Do you have any questions for us?
100. Is there anything else you'd like us to know?
